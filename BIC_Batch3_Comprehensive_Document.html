Marking Logic for B2B Advanced AI Course

For this 8-week online certification program, there are 8 quizzes and 8 assignments (4 descriptive and 4 programming).

  • The best 6 quizzes out of 8 will be considered. Each quiz carries 10 marks.
  • The best 2 programming assignments out of 4 will be considered. Each programming assignment carries 20 marks.
  • The marks for the descriptive assignments will not be considered in the evaluation.
  • The capstone project carries 100 marks.
