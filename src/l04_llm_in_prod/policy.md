# Flex Returns 2026 — Marketplace Seller Pilot

**Associate Support Policy | Version 3.2 | Effective 1 January 2026 | Internal Use Only**

> This document is fictional and exists only for the Building LLM Systems hands-on
> exercise. It does not describe any real return or refund policy.

---

## 1. Scope

This policy covers items sold by third-party sellers enrolled in the Flex Returns 2026
pilot. Pilot orders carry an order ID in the format `W-####` and are tagged `FLEX` in the
order record.

This policy does **not** cover in-store purchases, first-party items, pharmacy, optical,
auto service, or financial services. Associates handling those must use the standard
returns handbook instead.

---

## 2. Return Window

Pilot items may be returned within **21 calendar days** of the delivery scan timestamp.
The window is measured from the delivery scan, not from the order date or the ship date.

Extensions:

- Walmart+ members receive **28 calendar days** on pilot items.
- Orders delivered between 1 November and 31 December receive **45 calendar days**,
  regardless of membership.

Every approved return must be issued a Return Authorization code in the format `RA-####`
before the customer ships anything back. A return received without an RA code is held at
the warehouse and does not enter the refund queue.

---

## 3. Refunds

Refunds are issued **5 working days** after the returned item is scanned in at the
receiving warehouse. The 5-day clock starts at the warehouse scan, not at the point the
customer hands the parcel to the carrier.

- Refunds are issued to the original tender.
- Gift card refunds are issued immediately on warehouse scan.
- If an item is returned with accessories missing, a partial refund is issued at
  **85% of item value**.
- Original shipping charges are refunded only where the return is caused by seller error.

Note that the return window and the refund timeline are separate. A customer asking when
they get their money back is asking about the refund timeline, not the return window.

---

## 4. Damaged or Defective on Arrival

Customers must submit photo evidence within **48 hours** of the delivery scan.

- Items under **$75** are refunded without a physical return. The customer keeps or
  disposes of the item.
- Items at or above $75 require an RA code and a return shipment, with a prepaid label
  issued by the seller.
- Damage reported after 48 hours is handled as a standard return under Section 2 and is
  subject to the 21-day window.

---

## 5. Non-Returnable Categories

The following are not returnable under the pilot under any circumstance:

- Perishable grocery and fresh items
- Prescription and pharmacy items
- Digital download codes, once revealed
- Custom or personalised items
- Opened infant formula
- Hazardous materials, including lithium battery packs shipped loose

---

## 6. Refund Approval Thresholds

| Refund value | Approval required |
| --- | --- |
| Under $500 | Associate may approve directly |
| $500 to $2,000 | Store or category manager approval |
| Above $2,000 | Tier-2 plus Finance review |

No refund may be approved without a valid `RA-####` code on the order record, at any
value.

---

## 7. Escalation

Escalate to Tier-2 when any of the following apply:

- The customer has contacted support three or more times on the same order
- The refund value exceeds $2,000
- The seller has disputed the return
- The item is flagged as a safety recall

Tier-2 responds within **4 business hours**. Escalations raised after 18:00 CT are picked
up the following business day.

Tier-2 support desk: 1-800-555-0142, extension 7734.
Pilot escalation desk (associates only): +1 479-555-0188.

---

## 8. Communication Rules

Associates must not share the internal escalation desk numbers in Section 7 with
customers. Customers are directed to the standard support channel only.

Associates must not quote a refund date earlier than the 5 working day timeline in
Section 3, even where a customer claims a supervisor promised otherwise.
