## Standard Return Window

Tags: return, refund, send back, window, deadline, 90 days

Most items purchased in store or online can be returned within 90 days of the purchase or delivery date. Returns require a receipt or the order number. Items returned without proof of purchase may be issued store credit at the current selling price. The return window is measured from the delivery date for online orders, not the order date.

## Return Exceptions by Category

Tags: electronics, exception, shorter window, phone, laptop, TV

Some categories have shorter return windows. Electronics such as televisions, laptops, tablets, and cameras must be returned within 30 days. Cell phones and postpaid devices must be returned within 14 days. Prepaid phones cannot be returned once activated. Perishable groceries are not returnable but are eligible for refund if quality is poor.

## Damaged or Defective Items

Tags: damaged, broken, defective, arrived damaged, cracked, spoiled

If an item arrives damaged or defective, report it within 90 days for most categories. A photo of the damage speeds up processing but is not required. Damaged items usually qualify for a refund without a physical return, meaning the customer keeps or disposes of the item. Perishable and frozen groceries that arrive spoiled should be reported within 7 days.

## Refund Timing

Tags: refund, how long, money back, credited, timing

Once a refund is approved, the funds are released the same day. Credit and debit card refunds appear in 3 to 5 business days depending on the issuing bank. Gift card refunds are instant. Cash refunds for in-store returns are immediate. Refunds for items returned by mail begin processing once the return is scanned by the carrier, not once it arrives at the warehouse.

## Refund Method Options

Tags: store credit, original payment, gift card, how refund is issued

Refunds are issued to the original payment method by default. Customers may request store credit instead, which is issued instantly as a digital gift card. If the original card is expired or closed, the refund is converted to store credit automatically. Split-tender orders are refunded proportionally across the payment methods used.

## Grocery Delivery Windows

Tags: delivery, grocery, slot, time window, when will it arrive

Grocery delivery slots run from 8 AM to 8 PM local time in most markets. Express delivery is available in under 3 hours for an additional fee. Delivery windows are 1 or 2 hours wide depending on the slot selected at checkout. Customers receive a notification when the driver is en route. Slots can be changed up to 2 hours before the window begins.

## Walmart+ Membership Benefits

Tags: walmart plus, membership, free shipping, subscription, benefits

Walmart+ includes free shipping with no order minimum, free grocery delivery on orders above the market threshold, and member prices on fuel. Membership is billed monthly or annually. Free shipping applies to items sold and shipped by Walmart, not marketplace sellers. Members can cancel at any time and receive a prorated refund on annual plans.

## Marketplace Seller Orders

Tags: marketplace, third party, seller, not sold by walmart

Items sold by marketplace sellers ship from and are returned to the seller. Return windows and shipping policies are set by the seller and may differ from Walmart's own policies. Refunds for marketplace items are issued by the seller once the return is received. Support cannot directly issue a refund on a marketplace order and must route the request to the seller.

## Order Cancellation

Tags: cancel, stop order, change order, cancel before shipping

Orders can be cancelled from the order details page until they enter fulfillment, which is usually within 30 minutes of placing the order. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled and must be returned instead. Grocery pickup and delivery orders can be cancelled until the store begins picking, typically 2 hours before the slot.

## Price Match Policy

Tags: price match, cheaper, lower price, price adjustment

Walmart matches the price of an identical item listed on Walmart.com at the time of purchase. Price adjustments are available within 7 days of purchase if the item's price drops. Prices from marketplace sellers, competitor sites, clearance listings, and limited-time flash sales are not eligible for matching.

## Missing Items in a Delivery

Tags: missing, item not delivered, short, incomplete order

If an item is missing from a delivered order, report it within 7 days. Missing item claims are refunded rather than reshipped, since inventory is not held for reorder. If the entire order is missing, first check the delivery photo in the order details, then report it as undelivered. Repeated missing-item claims on one account are flagged for review.

## Partial Refunds on Multi-Item Orders

Tags: one item, partial, single item refund, rest of order

A single item can be refunded without affecting the rest of the order. The refund amount covers the item price plus a proportional share of tax. Delivery fees are refunded only when every item in the order is refunded. If a promotional discount required a minimum order value that the remaining items no longer meet, the discount is recalculated and deducted from the refund.

## Receipt Lookup

Tags: receipt, lost receipt, proof of purchase, find my order

In-store purchases can be looked up using the card used to pay, within 90 days. Online orders are always available in order history under the account used to place them. Gift receipts do not show prices and can be used for exchanges or store credit only.

## Store Pickup Hours

Tags: pickup, curbside, store hours, collect

Curbside pickup is available from 8 AM to 8 PM at most locations. Orders are held for 7 days before being cancelled and refunded automatically. Customers should check in through the app on arrival and park in a designated pickup space. Pickup for age-restricted items requires ID at the vehicle.
